# Doctor Review and Approval

Doctor profile/review data is never printed in Report 1. It includes qualified-reviewer confirmation, doctor name, registration number, qualification, contact information, clinical observations, confirmed symptoms, confirmed Prakriti/Vikriti/Agni, assessment, comments, approved wellness advice, restrictions, follow-up date/instructions, progress monitoring, final comments, signature/sign-off, approval status, and approval date.

Medicines are doctor-owned records with medicine name, dosage, frequency, duration, instructions, and doctor approval status. If absent, reports show **Not provided**. AI never invents medicine names or prescriptions.
