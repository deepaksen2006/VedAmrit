# Validation and Safety Rules

Unknown or missing allergy, intolerance, restriction, condition, medication restriction, pregnancy information, or significant dietary restriction is **REVIEW REQUIRED**; an empty field is not proof that no restriction exists. Before finalization, food and lifestyle content is checked against allergies, medicine allergies, intolerances, avoided foods, dietary preference, conditions, doctor restrictions, medications, severe symptoms, and pregnancy-related information where applicable.

Agent 1 assessment values are cross-checked with recorded source answers. Condition-related content is escalated for doctor review. AI and doctor content are separated: AI material is labeled **AI-GENERATED — DOCTOR REVIEW REQUIRED**; confirmed material is labeled **DOCTOR APPROVED**. The system uses conservative educational language and does not diagnose or independently prescribe.
